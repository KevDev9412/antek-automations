What usually comes up before we start.

Straight answers to what most people ask first.

Most first automations take four to six weeks from the day we have your system access and sample documents. That covers scoping, building, testing on your own data, and a live trial with a person approving each result. It moves at your pace as much as ours, so the biggest thing that stretches a timeline is waiting on access or data.

Every automation is scoped and quoted after a short call, based on what it replaces. Most clients pay a one-time build fee, and we can split it into monthly payments if that suits you better. Every automation comes with 30 days of free support after it goes live. An ongoing support plan is recommended but never required.

A person on your team approves each result before it goes anywhere: an invoice before it posts, a reply before it sends. We set it up so the details are already pulled out and checked, so each review is a quick look. You decide who approves, and anything that looks off is flagged with the reason attached.

Only the person building your automation, and only for what the automation needs. We don't ask for broader access than the task requires, and you can revoke it whenever you like.

For the first 30 days after go-live, fixes and adjustments are on us. After that, a support plan covers fixes, small changes and monitoring, and we respond to any failure within one business day. Without a plan, you can still bring us back for fixes and changes, quoted as they come up.
