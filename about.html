Behind the Automations

Built out of necessity.

Our founder, Kevin, comes from a business background and is at home with computers and software. When he went looking for ways to make his own business run more efficiently, he learned to build automations for it, and they worked.

That turned into a real appetite for learning more. Other businesses were stuck on the same repetitive work he had been, so he started building automations for them too. That's Antek: automation for the day-to-day operations of small and mid-sized businesses, so the work takes less time and costs less.

How we actually work.

One piece at a time

We start with whatever manual work is costing you the most time, get it running, then move to the next. Not a company-wide rollout on day one.

Someone approves every result

Each result goes to a person on your team before it posts, sends or files. We set it up so the details are already pulled out and checked, which makes each review a quick look.

Only what we can stand behind

If we build it, we stand behind it: 30 days of free support, then a support plan if you want one. It's also why we don't take on more than we can stay on top of.

A straight answer either way

Sometimes the honest answer is that something isn't worth automating yet. We'll say that instead of building it anyway.

Still deciding if this is worth a conversation?

Tell us what's manual and slow. We'll give you a straight answer on whether it's worth automating.
