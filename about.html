What Antek actually is, and what it isn't.

Not a software platform, and not an agency with layers between you and the work. A small operation that builds the automation and stays around to run it.

Where this started.

Antek started by automating one kind of manual work for one kind of business. What became clear fast: the problem was never specific to that business. Repetitive work costs the same hours everywhere, no matter what the company actually does. So the approach stayed the same, and the scope widened.

How this is different from a typical automation agency.

Not a claim to be better at everything. Just different in the parts that actually affect you.

How we actually work.

One piece at a time

We start with whatever manual work is costing you the most time, get it running, then move to the next. Not a company-wide rollout on day one.

Only what we can maintain

If we build it, we're the ones who keep it working. That's part of why we don't take on more than we can actually stay on top of.

A straight answer either way

Sometimes the honest answer is that something isn't worth automating yet. We'll say that instead of building it anyway.

Still deciding if this is worth a conversation?

Tell us what's manual and slow. We'll give you a straight answer on whether it's worth automating.
