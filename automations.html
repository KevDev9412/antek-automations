Watch the work. Skip the pitch.

Three automations, filmed running inside real businesses. No slides, no mockups, just the thing itself.

01

One inbox digest, sorted into everyone's morning before they've read it.

A single daily email carries the day's tasks in one long block of text. This automation opens it first, splits it into assignments for the right people, and loops in anyone else the work touches, drafting a note to an outside vendor on its own when a task depends on them.

02

A request lands, and it's already been answered.

This one keeps a quiet watch on the inbox for a single, specific kind of ask. The moment it shows up, the automation puts the requested document together and replies with it, the whole exchange finishes before anyone on staff would have even opened the message.

03

An invoice with the wrong numbers doesn't get paid by accident.

Every incoming invoice gets checked line by line against what was actually quoted, quantities, prices, anything billed for work that was never agreed to. A mismatch triggers a notice with the exact variance and a one-click way to approve or reject it, so a routine-looking invoice can't get approved by mistake.

In the video, we reject a live invoice and watch the automation draft the vendor email on its own, ready to send or hold for review.
