An invoice with the wrong numbers gets spotted instantly.

Every invoice line reconciled against the quote.

Every incoming invoice gets checked against what was actually quoted, quantities, prices, anything billed for work that was never agreed to. A mismatch triggers a notice with the exact variance and a one-click way to approve or reject it, so a routine-looking invoice can't get approved by mistake.

In a live setup, someone on your team makes that call on every flagged invoice, and nothing posts until they do.

In the video, we reject a flagged invoice and the system drafts the vendor email on its own, ready to send or hold for review.
